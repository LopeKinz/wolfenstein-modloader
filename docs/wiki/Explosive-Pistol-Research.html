In plain wordsIn einfachen Worten

This page traces the chain of decl data files behind the player's pistol in Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus: weapon, ammo, projectile and damage. It then describes a small mod that lets the pistol's projectile inherit from the rocket of the Kampfpistole, so every shot explodes, and raises the magazine to 30. The mod is structurally valid, but it has not been confirmed in the game.

  • The pistol is a chain of four decls: weapon, ammo (10 rounds by default), projectile and damage (20).
  • All three ammo upgrades (silencer, elephant rounds and both combined) inherit from the base projectile, so they would explode too.
  • The rocket's area damage is 250 in a radius of 2, and you take 25% self-damage.
  • The mod changes 3 assets: projectile inheritance, magazine 10 → 30, and the magazine upgrade 20 → 30.
  • Open in the game: whether the normal weapon class fires the rocket correctly, and whether enemies now react to every shot like a grenade.

Technical detailsTechnische Details

Der technische Teil ist auf Englisch – so, wie er im Wiki steht. Unterstrichene Begriffe haben eine Erklärung auf Deutsch.

Status as of 2026-09-25, read from Retail installation (read-only only). All data resides in gameresources.resources (Container MAIN, always mounted); deviations are noted below. Verifiable via: python tools/verify_weapon_mod.py. Status: structurally valid, unconfirmed in-game.

Chain of the Player Pistol

weapon/player/sp/handgun           inherit handgun_nomesh; Model pistole61.md6, magazineEnt, casing
weapon/player/sp/handgun_nomesh    inherit base_nomesh (no inventoryClass = Standard class)
                                   initialAmmoDecl = ammo/player/sp/handgun
                                   BURSTMODE_SINGLE, burstInterval 85, dualwieldable, maxCount 2
                                   upgrades: magazine_size, silencer, elephantrounds, elephantrounds_silencer
ammo/player/sp/handgun             inherit base_ammo (clipSize 30 as default)
                                   clipSize 10, maxCount 80, lowAmmoInClip 3, 20 ammoCountMeshes
                                   projectileDecl = projectile/player/pistol
projectile/player/pistol           inherit projectile/player/default  -> Hitscan (no hitscan=false)
                                   damageDecl bullet_pistol, impactEffectTable handgun, tracers 2
damage/firearm/bullet_pistol       inherit bullet_standard; 20/20, DAMAGETYPE_HANDGUN,
                                   declGoreBehavior standard/pistol

Upgrades (applyTo = weapon/player/sp/handgun) swap the projectile via modifiersData.item[].data.valueDecl: silencer -> pistol_silenced, elephantrounds -> pistol_elephant (Damage bullet_pistol_elephant 30, Gore standard/magnum; newer version in patch_2), elephantrounds_silencer -> pistol_elephantrounds_silenced. All three inherit (directly or via pistol_elephant) from projectile/player/pistol. magazine_size: AMT_AMMO_CLIP_SIZE, valueInt = 20, priority 4, without opType; located in gameresources and patch_3 (there additionally DMT_UPGRADE_ANIM). All four Retail magazine upgrades set exactly double the base magazine (10/20, 30/60, 20/40, 45/90) - whether "set" or "add" is intended, the data does not specify.

Decl entries in IDCL carry 0 dependencies; references are resolved at runtime by name. generated/buildgame/common.mapresources (highest copy in patch_4) loads the entire chain with the Always bit.

What Explodes

Every Retail projectile with splashDamageDecl is an Entity projectile (hitscan = false + notHitscanInfo.entityDef); a hitscan with area damage does not exist. Candidates: Grenades, dieselgun, ubergun, dkw_*, Combat Pistol.

Combat Pistol (ammo/player/sp/kampfpistol, 6 shots):

  • projectile/player/kampf_pistol - Grenade, parabolic trajectory, bounces off surfaces, ignites after 2.5s or upon contact with enemies (explodeOnActorsAfterTime 200).
  • Launcher upgrade weapons/kampf/launcher -> projectile/player/kampf_pistol_rocket: gravityFactor 0, speed 70, Entity projectile_ent/kampf_grenade_rocket (idProjectile_Grenade, explodeOnImpactAfterTime 75 ms, fuse 3s), splashDamageDecl damage/player/kampf_grenade: 250 damage, Radius 2 (inner 1), selfDamageScale 0.25, Gore standard/grenade + radial. Direct hit damage/player/kampf_grenade/minorimpact.

The Mod (mods/tnc_waffe_explosivpistole)

Asset Change Location
projectile:projectile/player/pistol inherit default -> kampf_pistol_rocket 408/448 B
ammo:ammo/player/sp/handgun edit.clipSize 10 -> 30 465/512 B
upgrade:weapons/handgun/magazine_size patch_3-Retail without indentation, valueInt 20 -> 30 439/448 B (gameresources), 439/512 B (patch_3)

Why use inherit instead of projectileDecl for the ammunition: The upgrades replace the projectile, but all replacement projectiles inherit from projectile/player/pistol. This causes basic, silencer, and elephant ammunition to explode, while the pistol retains its bullet sound, spread, and its hit damage (bullet_pistol instead of minorimpact); additionally, it gains the area damage of the combat pistol rocket.

Why the upgrade is a file: Installation.read reads the first copy (gameresources) and apply writes the result to every copy. A set would have taken DMT_UPGRADE_ANIM from the patch_3 copy. The indented patch_3 version packs to 467 bytes; the space in gameresources is 448 bytes—thus a rebuild of 702 MB. Without indentation (token-identical, as confirmed by the reviewer): 439 bytes.

Retail examples: The hatchet (weapon/player/sp/hatchet, also standard class) fires an entity projectile (axe_throw, hitscan = false). A magazine larger than ammoCountMeshes.num is common (smg 45/1, battlerifle 30/3).

Open (only to be clarified in-game)

  • Whether the standard weapon class starts the rocket entity exactly like idKampfpistol (the hatchet example supports this).
  • Whether the spawned entity takes damage from projectile/player/pistol or from its own projectileDecl (kampf_pistol_rocket) — it should explode in both cases.
  • Setting or adding AMT_AMMO_CLIP_SIZE: with magazine upgrade 30 or 60.
  • The rocket does not ignite on impact under ~5 m (75 ms at speed 70), but bounces off and ignites later — self-damage 25 %.
  • aiEventDecl base/misc/grenade: Enemies react to every shot as if it were a grenade; the silencer no longer makes the pistol silent.
