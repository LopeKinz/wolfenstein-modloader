In plain wordsIn einfachen Worten

This page is a table of contents for the research notes on Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus, Wolfenstein: The New Order and related work. It links the task briefs (what a researcher was asked to find out) and the reports (what they found), now including every report of research rounds 2 to 10, plus pages on building a new map from existing parts, missions, custom map prototypes, DLC tests and weapons. The notes keep the original measurements, failed attempts and open questions. It contains no results of its own; it helps you find the right page.

  • 24 research briefs describing tasks, e.g. encryption keys, mesh and collision formats.
  • 93 research reports with the results: 36 from the early work and 57 from rounds 2 to 10.
  • Rounds 6 to 10 add 24 reports, e.g. on The New Order's decrypted code, engine limits, the developer menu and new map names.
  • Extra sections for kit-based map building, mission research, map prototypes, DLC tests and weapons.
  • Failed approaches and uncertainty are kept, not smoothed over.
