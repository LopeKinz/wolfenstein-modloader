In plain wordsIn einfachen Worten

This page lists the remaining 1,668 of The New Order's cvars: vehicles (vehicle_), network (net_), the engine core (com_), sound (s_), menus (swf_), input and many smaller groups. Each row shows the type, the default value, its status (cheat, normal, saved, start-up-only or read-only), the allowed range where the game defines one, and the developers' own description.

  • 1,668 cvars; the largest groups are vehicle_ (223), net_ (132), pb_ (125), com_ (79), s_ (73) and swf_ (58).
  • 1,496 are cheats.
  • Read-only and start-up-only settings, such as com_headless or com_multiplayer, are marked in the status column.
  • Prefixes with fewer than 10 cvars share one table at the end of the page.
