In plain wordsIn einfachen Worten

This page lists the large raw files produced by the reverse-engineering work, which stay in the code repository instead of the wiki because they are too big. It notes that nine more research rounds (2 to 10) have landed since the inventory was made; it links the reports of rounds 2 to 5 itself and points to the Reverse Engineering Research index for all of them. It notes that this research is static (reading files and code) unless a page says a change was confirmed in the running game.

  • Inventory size at generation time: 1,918 files, 7.4 GiB in total.
  • The biggest parts are game archives (.resources, 3.8 GiB) and texture databases (.texdb, 2.5 GiB).
  • Round 2 has 10 reports, round 3 has 11, round 4 has 11 and round 5 has 1; rounds 6 to 10 are listed on the Reverse Engineering Research index.
  • The console unlock and the Kiscule Command node are written up with in-game proof on the TNC Console Unlock page.

Technical detailsTechnische Details

Der technische Teil ist auf Englisch – so, wie er im Wiki steht. Unterstrichene Begriffe haben eine Erklärung auf Deutsch.

The Wiki includes every prose research note from re_probes as an English page. Large generated artifacts remain in the repository's re_probes/ tree: copying multi-gigabyte .resources, .texdb, images, archives, and temporary failures into a GitHub Wiki would duplicate data and exceed GitHub file-size limits.

Inventory at generation time: 1,918 files, 7.4 GiB total.

Newest Research (added 2026-10-01)

Nine more rounds of research landed since this inventory was generated — their reports live in re_probes/agent_reports/ (r2_*.md through r10_modtestmap.md) and are published as their own Research-Report-R<round>-<Topic> pages; Reverse Engineering Research lists all of them. Rounds 2 to 5:

  • Round 2: Missionhud, Mapnodlc, Ainav, Tilechain, Md6anim, Tnomodels, Wwisereplace, Videoreplace, Newassets, Maplightstairs
  • Round 3: Animdecode, Tileinner, Tnoskinned, Newassetsv, Mapselfcontained, Missionsetter, Newweapon, Npcswap, Swfui, Kisculeref, Navdata
  • Round 4: Console-Commands, Model-Import, Strings, Pickups, Ai-Decls, Savegame, Materials, Tno-Custom-Maps, Sound-Events, Ai-Spawn-Waves, Integrity
  • Round 5: Kiscule-Command

All of it is static (disassembly/container reading over a snapshot or the files on disk), except where a page explicitly says a change was confirmed by starting the real game. Two topics from this batch — the console/Cvar restriction (round 4, Console-Commands) and the Kiscule Command node that reaches it from inside a map script (round 5, Kiscule-Command) — are written up together with their in-game confirmation on TNC Console Unlock; the full round-3 Kiscule node catalogue (Kisculeref, re_probes/kisculeref/catalogue.json, see the new kisculeref row below) is written up on Kiscule Node Reference. Both pages keep the PROVEN/INFERRED split explicit rather than flattening it.
