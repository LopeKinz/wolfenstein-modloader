In plain wordsIn einfachen Worten

This report describes a new small level, 'Atriumhall', built from existing wall, floor and prop pieces to replace a DLC map (c2v1) in Wolfenstein II (TNC). A design tool places the pieces plus enemies, pickups and lights, and automatic checks confirm the layout is walkable, closed and fits the original map's footprint. The level is structurally valid, but none of it has been loaded in the game yet.

  • Layout: entrance, an 8.5 m tall atrium with a red monolith and gallery ring, a corridor, two offices and a courtyard.
  • The latest build has 927 kit placements plus 9 enemies, 12 pickups and 12 lights.
  • Enemies stand only where the original navigation mesh exists, because it can only be reused.
  • All checks pass, and 13 deliberately broken layouts are caught.
  • Open: enemies have no spawners, there is no level end without a new script, and nothing is tested in the game.

Summary

The level is newly built and replaces the contents of game/dlc/c02/c2v1. It consists of:

  • a Windfang serving as the player spawn area
  • an 8.5 m high atrium hall with a red monolith and a gallery ring at 0 m
  • a two-sided staircase
  • a green-lit corridor
  • two offices
  • an open courtyard with a locked door

Only kit parts from re_probes/kitmap/kit/kit.json are used: 915 placements from 26 models. This is supplemented by 4 custom boxes (Monolith, Base, 2 Obelisks). The boxes carry a CLIPMODEL_BOX and therefore do not require cm files.

Placed items include:

  • 3 player spawns: the two retained from the Skeleton and the recommended clone, all at the same location
  • 9 enemies
  • 12 pickups
  • 12 lights
  • 1 invisible "locked" hint

All tests are green, all 13 control samples pass, and the 1.0 m positive control succeeds.

The Flow (approx. 3–6 minutes)

  1. Windfang (10 × 4 m, −4.5 m): The player starts facing North. In front lies a 2 m wide passage; to the left and right are ammunition and armor, with cold-white lighting. Here, the player is safe.
  2. Atrium hall (20 × 28 m, −4.5 m, 8.5 m high): The line of sight leads to the red monolith. It is 7 m high, stands on a black base over the floor mosaic, and is flanked by two black obelisks. Two red strip lights illuminate it from below. Above the hall runs a gallery ring at 0 m around an open air space of 12 × 20 m.
    • Below the hall: two light soldiers and one heavy soldier under the North gallery.
    • Above: the Nagler on the West gallery and a light soldier on the East gallery. They shoot down into the hall.
  3. Staircase: Two sides along the East edge of the air space, −4.5 → Podest −2.25 → 0 m. At the top, one arrives at the North gallery. MP ammunition is located at the staircase, MP at the West end.
  4. Corridor (4 × 20 m, 0 m): The door in the North wall of the gallery leads into a hallway with pale green lighting. Side doors lead to both offices.
  5. West Office with warm lighting: one heavy soldier, large medikit, helmet. East Office with cold lighting: one combat dog and the combat rifle.
  6. Courtyard (20 × 20 m, open, moonlight): It is accessible via three doors, from the corridor and from both offices. This creates a loop: Office – Courtyard – Office – Corridor.
    • Enemies: the Overseer with Cannon and an Elite soldier.
    • Cover: four columns and crates.
    • On the North wall hangs a static door under red alarm light. It bears a "locked" hint; retail has no door in c2v1 that opens.

There is no level end. The Skeleton removes mission scripts, so it is missing until a new script is written.

Why the hall is an atrium with a gallery ring

The Navmesh can only be reused (see mapmanifest.md). Enemies run only where the retail floor existed. The retail collision for c2v1 shows a ring exactly at 0 m above the lobby surrounding an air space (x −5…7, y −190,5…−170) with no floor above it.

The first version had only a North Gallery. The new navmesh_islands measurement showed there 163 m² of "Phantom Floor": retail Navmesh at 0 m where the new level has air. Enemies viewed from above would want to "run" across the hall. With the ring, that is 3 m², a 0.5–1 m wide remainder behind the railings.

The hall therefore deliberately follows the retail ring. Everything else is new floor plan:

  • Windfang
  • Monolith Axis
  • Staircase in the air space
  • Corridor
  • Offices
  • Courtyard

These rooms lie where retail had completely different walls.

Footprint and Heights

  • The entire level was shifted 1 m west compared to the first draft. The offices begin at y −162 and the corridor walls stand inside. The reason: the retail footprint (floor at any height) ends at x 12 (lobby) and at x 11 (north block). Between them, it has only a 4 m wide neck at y −166…−163 (x −1…3).
  • Now every part, every box, and every entity lies above retail floor. The list outside_retail_footprint is empty, and everything lies within the retail core bbox.
  • Floor heights: −4.5 m has 546.8 m² of retail floor in the level rectangle, 0 m has 763.3 m². The platform at −2.25 m is explicitly only for the player (PLAYER_ONLY_HEIGHTS). No enemy stands there; this is checked.

Tests

python tools/design_kitmap.py builds, checks, and writes the output in approximately 25 seconds; the result is OK: 0 failure(s), negatives all caught. It checks the following, and each point fails if it is incorrect:

  • No two wall parts (wall, cover, jamb/post/overhang, door leaf, railing, columns, own boxes) overlap in volume; floors do not overlap with each other; props do not intersect walls.
  • Every cell of every room (0,1-m grid, 0.25 m inset) has a floor part at room height.
  • Every room is reachable from the player start for an 0.8 m wide body; stairs count as ramps, steps are at most 0.35 m high. This allows passing every gap ≥ 1.0 m and prevents gaps ≤ 0.8 m.
  • Player start, enemies, and pickups stand on a floor part and are reachable.
  • Everything lies within the retail core bbox and above the retail floor footprint; floor heights are retail floor heights.
  • Every enemy stands on retail-free floor of its height, with ≥ 6 m² contiguous area in the room.
  • Every part has a cm at its scale.
  • 6–12 lights and 6–10 enemies; no duplicate names.
  • Game cross-section, read-only:
    • The defs of all 41 entities (including own boxes) are mounted entityDefs; all donors exist in retail c2v1 with the same def.
    • Frame openings exist in the cm (cuts at z 1.0 and at the overhang).
    • Materials and own boxes are correct.
  • Skeleton fallback path: If retail world.hkshape remains, 6 of its invisible blockers are in our rooms: on Gallery West, Gallery South, in the corridor, and 3 areas above head height. All rooms and entities remain reachable nonetheless.

For the Master: reading layout.json

  • pieces[]: kit (model name from kit.json), pos, yaw (degrees), scale, aabb, room, role. Each part becomes an idStaticInstanceEntity (inherits func/staticinstance) after the example_entity from kit.json. Formula: World = t + x·A0 + y·A1 + z·A2, A_i = scale_i · mat[i], mat[0] = (cos, sin, 0), mat[1] = (−sin, cos, 0).
  • authored[]: own boxes with model name, material, box dimensions, generating properties and the alternate path.
  • entities[]: def, class, pos, yaw, properties (spawnPosition, spawnOrientation, light color/intensity/radius or target), donor (retail block as template) and drop_refs.
  • checks: This section contains all check results:
    • navmesh_proxy and navmesh_islands
    • retail_hkshape_fallback
    • game.retail_door_relation
    • the negative tests

Risks and open points

  • Unconfirmed at runtime. Nothing has been loaded in the game.
  • Enemies without spawners and without scripts (hide = false): Whether they perceive and attack is only shown by testing. Retail sets all enemies in c2v1 via spawners.
  • Navmesh:
    • The obstacle and phantom counts are an offline substitute from retail collision (0.5m grid), not the real Navmesh.
    • Enemies can get stuck on new obstacles, especially on the lower staircase.
    • The sergeant has 609 m² of Navmesh beyond our walls. He could run against the courtyard wall if he does not reach the player.
  • New model entries (own boxes) require a tool that adds entries to the container. Such a tool does not yet exist; currently, only rebuild replaces them. The alternative is the replacement path models/editor/box.lwo.
  • megatexturedModel: All retail instances have true. Whether new instance names need data for this is open (kit risk).
  • Blocker: Does the master retain retail world.hkshape? There are 3 invisible crates on walkable areas, on Gallery West, Gallery South, and in the corridor. The path remains free everywhere, but one bumps into them. Exchanging against c06p2 from the Skeleton report is recommended.
  • No level end without a new mission script.
