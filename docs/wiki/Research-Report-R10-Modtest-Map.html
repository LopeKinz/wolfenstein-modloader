This round-10 report builds a developer map for trying out mods in Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus, named game/wolfsdk/modtest. Instead of a new room it copies the retail Gestapo office map (c2v1), because that already has working collision, lighting and the walkable navmesh enemies need; the mission is removed and test content is added. The build keeps every existing entry byte-identical and passes 79 of 79 checks. The report itself lists everything as not yet proven in game.

  • 304 of 9,206 entities are removed (mission scripts, hidden enemies, triggers, alarms, retail pickups); 8,902 stay unchanged.
  • The armoury gets 23 pickups (11 weapons plus ammo, health and armour) and 5 lights; the office hall gets 8 enemies with their backs to the door.
  • The new name needs 18 map-level files and 102 light-probe images copied; 120 entries are added to the archive.
  • The LKW and DKW weapons and grenades have no pickup in the game; they can only come from the developer loadout, which may not apply on the devmap path.
  • Not proven in game at the time: enemies without a spawner, collision of 91 shared shapes, pickup heights and the light probes; a robot enemy and respawns were left out.

Round-10 pass: a developer map for testing mods, built on retail c2v1 geometry and added as a further map to the same dlc_0 chunk that already holds the kit level and the box map. The build keeps every kit-level entry byte-identical and passes all its checks. Not proven in game; the report lists what is unproven, worst first, and gives the install steps and the in-game test.

Date: 2026-10-02. The game was not started. Nothing was written under <Steam library> or Saved Games; the install was only read. Builds went only to D:/wolf-decomp/tmp/modtestmap/. New files: tools/build_modtestmap.py and this report. No other file was changed. tools/build_boxmap.py and wolfsdk/mapinstall.py were reused without edits.

How it composes with the box: build_modtestmap.run() calls build_boxmap.run() (H → H + box, all box checks), then adds modtest's entries to that chunk with the same build_kitmap.rebuild() and idclwrite.finalize(). Nothing in the box code is duplicated (maplevel(), ENGINE_NAMES, DEVMENU and clone logic are imported).

Design choice: retail c2v1 geometry, not a kit/glTF room

Chosen: copy the RETAIL c2v1 map (dlc_2's chunk_22) under the new name. The mission is removed and our test content is added.

Why:

  • Enemies need a navmesh. One cannot be generated (r3_navdata.md §6: no encoder; the engine only cuts holes, it never adds surface). A kit or glTF room has no navmesh under it, so AI would stand still at best. Retail c2v1 ships c2v1.hknavmesh. The build copies it byte-identical (gated) and checks every enemy against it.
  • Collision is solved there. The player walks on retail's playerclip carrier mesh (memory: tnc-custom-map-route, variant D analysis). New kit geometry needed the | 0x18 cm workaround and still had stair problems.
  • Lighting is solved there. Baked lightmaps (.proc), 102 light probes, 408 retail lights and the Umbra tome/pvs/shadows all match the geometry they were baked for. The kit/glTF route borrows single lightmap texels (r9 risk 5: "blotchy or dark").
  • Scene points can stay. The 176 combat, 54 roam, 41 search and 10 traversal scene points belong to the retail geometry. Enemies keep cover and search behaviour. A new room has none (r2_ainav.md §5).
  • Precedent: a retail-based c2v1 container served from dlc_0 loaded and played in game on 2026-09-24 (relit lights, normal mission start, memory tnc-custom-map-route). The kit/glTF route has no in-game AI evidence.

Cost: the map is the Gestapo office, not a clean white room. The test area uses two of its rooms (below). The rest of the building stays walkable and empty.

What the new name needs (and gets)

  • 18 map-level entries of retail c2v1, copied under the new name:
    • .entities and .mapresources (both our own payload);
    • _combo/modtest.proc, modtest.prockd;
    • _world.tome + -metadata.json, _world.bmodel, sky.bmodel, world.hkshape, _world.pvs, _world.bshadows;
    • modtest.ambientsh, modtest.hknavmesh, devmapinfo.json;
    • mega.decl, megatrans.decl, megafoliagetrans.decl;
    • generated/staticstreamtree/game/wolfsdk/modtest.bsstree.

These are retail bytes. H's modified tome and hkshape are not used, and H's removed _world.bshadows is taken from retail. - 102 light-probe images under maps/game/wolfsdk/modtest/lightprobes/<entity>_row.bimage. The engine builds that name from the map name ('maps/%s/lightprobes/%s_row.bimage', r9 #7). The box has no probes, so it did not need this; modtest keeps all 102 LIGHT_PROBE entities. - All 15 engine-derived names exist under the new name: the box's 14 format strings plus _combo/_world.bshadows. - Per-entity resources stay under c2v1's names. These are the 90 trigger/volume/collision-only cms; the text names them explicitly (clipModelName). Every such reference is checked to resolve. - mega*.decl keeps landpagefile on c2v1's VT pages (r9 #9). - textures.cache is one more hardlink, dlc_0/base/maps/game/wolfsdk/modtest/textures.cache. mapinstall.install already creates it for every extra map.

How the donors were chosen:

  • Each donor is the first retail placement without a layer that has isStatic = false, so physics settles it. If no such placement exists, it is the first placement without a layer. The scan runs over base/*.resources, then dlc_1..dlc_3, in name order.
  • Static pickups get z + 0.03 (retail rests them at 0.02–0.04 in c2v1). Dynamic ones get z + 0.10 and drop onto the floor.

Mapresources

  • Retail rows are kept in order, the map-named ones renamed, plus the 34 rows.

Install side

  • mapinstall.check_container returns ['game/wolfsdk/box', 'game/wolfsdk/modtest'].
  • tools/verify_mapinstall.py passes ("Alles in Ordnung").

Determinism: two builds give the same sha256, and the box stage passes its own determinism check.

Not proven in game (worst first)

  1. The dev menu command branch (tnc_devmenu_fix), devmap with a non-retail name, and the box itself have never run (r9 "Not proven" 1–2). modtest depends on the same chain.
  2. Enemies placed with hide = false and no spawner have no in-game evidence yet. The precedents are the kit map and a c2v2 debug dummy. They should idle and then fight, using the retail scene points, but this is unseen.
  3. Campaign mode with no chapter part makes the player the generic player. Whether devmapInvLoadout (all weapons) is applied on the devmap path is still open (r4_pickups open question 1). The pickups work either way.
  4. Collision of 91 shared cms: H's variant-D fix ORed 0x18 into 91 model-named cms that retail c2v1 also uses. Those cms are shared by name and cannot change without changing c2v1. Retail static instances with these models (floors, walls, caps, bookcases, chairs) now also block the player and AI.
    • Expected effect: none on the floors.
    • Possible effect: snagging on stairs. The test area has none.
  5. Pickup heights. A static pickup from another map may float or sink a few centimetres. Its rest height was taken from c2v1's measurements.
  6. Sound. There are no map sound banks: expect the warning [SOUND] No banks found for map 'game/wolfsdk/modtest'. Ambience is silent; weapons and VO use global banks.
  7. Light probes. The probes' images are byte copies under the new name. That the engine finds them there is read, not seen.

Install (game closed; Freedom Chronicles installed)

  1. Revert the mods in the loader (or python -m wolfsdk revert). While mods are applied, mapinstall may only swap the chunk; the new textures.cache link is a new file.
  2. Install the chunk: python -m wolfsdk map-install D:\wolf-decomp\tmp\modtestmap\modtest\dlc_0\base\chunk_22.resources This writes dlc_0's packagemapspec with all four maps, hardlinks both new textures.cache names, and records everything for uninstall.
  3. Dev menu: add the item below to devmenuoption/main in tools/build_devmenu_fix.py (another agent owns that file). It goes after the box item: num 4 → 5. Without the box, use item[3] and num 3 → 4.

As a dict next to BOX_ITEM:

python MODTEST_ITEM = {"devMenuDisplayName": "WolfSDK: Mod test map", "cmds": ['si_spawnspot "modtest_start"', 'si_nextlayeractive "layer/dlc/c2v1"', 'si_filllayeractive ""', 'si_devprogression ""', 'si_checkpointName ""', "si_gamemode 0", "devmap game/wolfsdk/modtest"]} Then rebuild that mod. 4. Apply the mods again and start the game.

Undo: python -m wolfsdk map-uninstall, or install the H or box chunk again.

In-game test

  1. Dev menu → "WolfSDK: console test" must show g_debugLayerChanges = 1. Otherwise the command branch is broken and nothing below means anything.
  2. Dev menu → "WolfSDK: Mod test map".
    • Pass: you stand in a lit room with weapons, ammo, health and armour on the floor to the left and right. A door to the north leads into an office hall with 8 enemies standing with their backs to you.
  3. Pick up each weapon. Open the weapon wheel and check whether all weapons, including LKW/DKW, are there from the start (the loadout question).
  4. Walk through the door and shoot:
    • the soldier at 13 m;
    • the elite at 23 m;
    • the Übersoldat at the far end at 35 m.

Do they react, take cover behind the desks, and come after you? God mode from cheats_gamemode helps. 5. condump modtest1.txt and send the file.

Open (skipped for this version, per the coordinator)

  • Robot enemy (panzerhund): built and checked once, then left out as experimental.
    • It is retail c2v3 data in the same chunk, with the same mounts.
    • It needs the donor from c2v3's text plus 365 preload rows matched by name from c2v3's list. Every one of those rows resolved.
    • ROBOT in the builder is the ready entry: append it to ENEMIES and rebuild.
    • Its spot (24, −36.5) passed the 1.2 m navmesh-disc check.
  • Enemy respawn/reset:
    • Not built. No retail evidence says an idTarget_AISpawn can fire twice (r4_aispawnwaves §3).
    • A new kiscule decl entry is unproven.
    • Reloading the dev menu item is the reset.
  • Laserhund or other robots, and a second wave behind a trigger: not attempted.
  • LKW/DKW as world pickups: none exist in the game. They are only reachable through the dev loadout.
